Foods I avoid

  • Pork
  • Marbled or gristly meat (tendons, connective tissue, bone-in chicken)
  • Steak cooked less than medium well
  • Most fish, except salmon and shrimp
  • Mollusks
  • Runny eggs
  • Mushroom, except on pizza
  • Eggplant
  • Blue cheese and most other soft cheeses
  • Spicy foods, but I can handle up to buffalo sauce
  • Pickled foods
